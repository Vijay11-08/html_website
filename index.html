Best Practices

Why Semantic HTML Matters

Using meaningful HTML5 tags instead of generic <div> containers gives machines and humans clear understanding of your content hierarchy.

  • Accessibility (a11y): Screen readers allow visually impaired users to jump directly between landmarks.
  • SEO Ranking: Google's crawlers easily index high-value articles, headers, and metadata.
  • Maintainability: Clean markup reduces development and maintenance overhead.

Non-Semantic vs Semantic HTML5 Modern Standard
❌ Non-Semantic (Div Soup) 
<div class="header">
  <div class="nav">...</div>
</div>
<div class="content">
  <div class="post">
    <div class="title">...</div>
  </div>
</div>
<div class="footer">...</div>
✅ Semantic HTML5 
<header>
  <nav>...</nav>
</header>
<main>
  <article>
    <h1>...</h1>
  </article>
</main>
<footer>...</footer>
